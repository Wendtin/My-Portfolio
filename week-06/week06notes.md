## Overview

Week 06 was the Phase 1 midterm assessment. The practical exam
required applying skills accumulated across weeks 01–05 in an
integrated scenario. Deliverables included a System Architecture
Document, a readiness check log, and a practical exam report
demonstrating command over Linux, networking, scripting, containers,
and Active Directory concepts.

## Challenges

Performing under exam conditions required confident recall of commands
and concepts without reference materials. Time management across the
multiple deliverable components was the primary challenge, resolved by
prioritizing the SAD document first and completing the practical report
from structured notes taken during the exam.
