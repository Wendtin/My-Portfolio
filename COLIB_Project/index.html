COLIB is a free online library designed to foster a love for reading and community interaction.
It offers a diverse selection of books and newspapers, accessible to local readers. The platform
organizes publications by genre and highlights paperback books, recommending a featured book
each week through its cover page. COLIB aims to promote literacy and build connections through
shared reading experiences.
