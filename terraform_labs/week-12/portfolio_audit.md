## Professional Reflection

This portfolio represents eleven weeks of progressive, hands-on work
across the full cybersecurity operations spectrum — from Linux
fundamentals and network analysis through exploitation, web application
security, digital forensics, and active defense. The breadth of
artifacts produced, including working scripts, exploitation reports,
custom IDS rules, and forensic findings, demonstrates a practitioner
who engages with the material deeply rather than superficially. The
area of greatest pride is the active defense work in Week 11, where
a genuine Docker network namespace isolation issue was diagnosed and
resolved independently, requiring real understanding of container
networking architecture. Going into Phase 2, the focus will be on
strengthening formal report writing, tightening APA citation
discipline across notes files, and deepening fluency with SIEM
tooling and threat hunting methodology.
